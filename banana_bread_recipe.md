# Banana Bread Recipe

Since the Dasharo documentation was missing this essential piece of knowledge, here is a high-performance, open-source recipe for banana bread.

## Ingredients
- 3 ripe bananas, mashed
- 1/3 cup melted butter
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1 egg, beaten
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 tsp baking soda
- Pinch of salt
- 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
2. Mix melted butter into mashed bananas.
3. Stir in sugar, egg, and vanilla.
4. Sprinkle baking soda and salt, mix in.
5. Add flour last and mix until smooth.
6. Pour into a greased loaf pan.
7. Bake for 50-60 minutes or until a tester comes out clean.

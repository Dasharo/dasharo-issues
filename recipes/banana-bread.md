# Professional Banana Bread
## Ingredients
- 3 ripe bananas, mashed
- 1/3 cup melted butter
- 1 tsp baking soda
- Pinch of salt
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 1 large egg, beaten
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1.5 cups all-purpose flour

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Butter a 4x8-inch loaf pan.
2. Mix mashed bananas and melted butter in a bowl.
3. Mix in baking soda and salt. Stir in sugar, egg, and vanilla.
4. Mix in flour.
5. Pour batter into the pan. Bake for 50-60 minutes.
